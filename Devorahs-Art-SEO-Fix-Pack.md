This is the highest-impact fix. A clean homepage is what makes Google and visitors take the site seriously.

**Where:** WordPress admin → Pages → Home → Edit with Elementor.

### 1a. Delete the fake book section

Find the block near the top that says "New Release / Before You Say Goodbye / A Fiction Novel By Thomas Dietrich" with the award badges and book image. It is leftover template junk. Delete that whole section (in Elementor, hover the section, click the grey handle, choose Delete).

Keep the main title area, but use this real copy.

**Sub-line:**
> Original acrylic portraits by Devorah, a young Israeli artist. Each piece is a high-resolution digital download, ready to print and hang at home.

**Button text:** Shop the collection → links to /shop/

Where the page currently says "Discover a collection of digital art inspired by the beauty of everyday life...", paste this instead:

Google can't "see" images, it reads the alt text. Right now the images likely have none. For each product image, set alt text in the WordPress Media Library (click the image, fill the "Alternative Text" box). Use plain descriptions with a keyword:

- Colorful Confidence → "Colorful anime-style portrait of a girl in orange, green and blue"

## PART 5 — How free traffic actually comes (and what to do next)

On-site SEO (Parts 1 to 4) is the foundation. Google now has real words to match people's searches to. But a brand-new small shop won't rank overnight. Here's the honest path, in priority order.

**1. Get the site indexed properly (free, 20 min, do it once).**
Set up Google Search Console (search.google.com/search-console), add the site, and submit the sitemap. This tells Google every page exists. I can walk you through this step by step whenever you're ready — it's the single most important free traffic step after fixing the pages.

**2. Pinterest is where art buyers actually browse (free, biggest lever for you).**
Pinterest works like a search engine for visual things, and "digital wall art / printable art" is one of its most-searched categories. Each painting becomes a pin that links straight back to its product page. This is the fastest realistic source of relevant traffic for art like Devorah's. You said socials are unsure — Pinterest is the one I'd open first, above Instagram, because pins keep bringing traffic for months, not hours. Say the word and I'll set up the account plan and write the first 8 pins.

**3. Let the paintings themselves do content marketing.**
Every time Devorah finishes a piece, a short "here's how this came together" post (using the studio photos) is free content Google and Pinterest both reward. This is the RSS/content angle — a simple rhythm of new work + behind-the-scenes.
