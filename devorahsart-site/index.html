What is Devorah's Art?

Devorah's Art is an online gallery of original hand-painted portraits sold as affordable digital downloads. Every piece on devorahsart.com is painted by Devorah, a young Israeli artist, and delivered as a high-resolution file you print at home.

Unlike print-on-demand shops that sell the same stock image to thousands of buyers, each piece here starts as an original drawing or painting, mostly created at home. Subjects range from everyday commute scenes and emotional portraits to playful character studies and tender nursery art. The site exists so anyone, anywhere, can own original human-made art starting at $3, without shipping delays or gallery markups.

How much does printable digital art cost on this site?

Traditional hand-drawn and painted scans cost $5. Digital compositions cost $3. Any 3 prints in your cart are $10. There are no subscriptions, no hidden fees, and no shipping charges because nothing physical is mailed with a download.

Prices are set for students, first apartments, nurseries, and gift-givers who want real art without a $200 tag. PayPal handles checkout. After purchase, Devorah emails the high-resolution file within 24 hours. Print a 5×7 for a desk, an 11×14 for a bedroom, or larger if your printer or local shop supports it. Original paintings and custom portraits, when offered, are $30 to $60.

What style of art does Devorah paint?

Devorah paints expressive acrylic portraits influenced by anime, pop art, and classical figure painting. Her brushwork is loose and emotional rather than photorealistic: bold color, honest feeling, everyday subjects.

You will find girls lost in headphones on a bus, fathers caught in quiet reflection, wild-haired characters full of energy, and small tender objects like teddy bears that carry nostalgia. The through-line is feeling: each piece captures a mood you recognize even if you have never met the subject. That mix of anime-influenced composition and painterly texture is what makes the work stand out in a crowded digital art market.

How do digital art downloads work?

Pick a portrait, pay $3 or $5 through PayPal (or $10 for any 3 in the cart), and receive a high-resolution file by email within 24 hours. Open the file on your computer or phone and send it to your home printer or a local print shop.

Digital downloads remove warehousing, shipping, and inventory costs, which is how original art stays at $3 to $5. The file is yours for personal printing and display. Most buyers use standard photo paper, cardstock, or premium matte paper depending on the room. Online printers like Printful or local shops can produce gallery-quality results if you want something larger than your home printer handles.

Why buy art directly from an independent young artist?

Independent artists rely on direct sales to keep painting. Devorah is mostly self-taught and works at home, with occasional studio visits; photos on the About page show her real process. Can I print Devorah's art at any size?

Yes. Each file is scanned in high resolution so you can print small (5×7 in) or large (18×24 in and beyond) depending on your printer or print shop.

Digital art downloads are popular precisely because scale is flexible. A nursery might want a soft 8×10; a dorm room might want a bold 16×20. Because you receive the master file, you are not locked into one size. For best results, use a reputable print shop for sizes above 11×14 and choose matte or semi-gloss paper for portrait work.

Anime-influenced portraits combine expressive faces, vivid color, and relatable characters, the same qualities that make illustration share well on Pinterest, Instagram, and TikTok.
