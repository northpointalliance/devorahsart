Frequently asked questions

How buying works: $5 traditional scans, $3 digital compositions, any 3 prints for $10, and 100% human-created art.

Is any art on this site made with AI?

No. Every image on Devorah's Art is 100% human-created: hand-drawn or hand-painted in studio, or painted by Devorah on her laptop. There are zero AI-generated images.

Traditional listings are scans of acrylic, graphite, or colored-pencil work on paper and canvas. Digital listings are compositions Devorah draws herself. The header on every page says Handmade. No AI. so buyers are not guessing.

How does the 3 for $10 offer work?

Add any 3 prints to your cart and the three highest-priced files are billed at $10 total. Remaining items stay at their list price of $5 or $3.

Example: three $5 scans cost $10 instead of $15. Four items that include three $5 scans and one $3 digital file cost $13. If three $3 digital files would cost $9 at list price, you still pay $9.

What do I get when I buy a piece?

You receive a high-resolution digital file by email within 24 hours of PayPal payment. The file is ready to print at home or through any print shop, at any size you choose.

Each purchase includes one master file scanned from Devorah's original painting. Print for personal use in as many sizes as you like for your own home, not for resale.

How do I pay for digital art on Devorah's Art?

Can I print the art more than once?

Yes, for personal use. Each purchase grants you the right to print and display the art in your own home, not to resell the file or use it commercially.

Many buyers print a backup copy or try two sizes before framing. That flexibility is a core benefit of digital downloads over single-size physical prints.

Does Devorah ship physical prints?

Downloads do not include shipping. Traditional scans are $5 and digital compositions are $3 so you can print locally. Original physical paintings and custom portrait commissions, when available, are $30 to $60.

If you want a shipped original or a commission, email after browsing the shop. For prints, you control paper, framing, and size at home.

What print sizes work best?

Files are high resolution, so common sizes from 5×7 in up to 18×24 in and larger work well. Match the size to the room: small for desks, medium for bedrooms, large for feature walls.

Home inkjet printers handle up to 8.5×11 or A4 easily. For bigger prints, use a local or online print service and upload the file they receive from you after email delivery.

Yes. At $3 to $5, or $10 for three prints, portraits make easy gifts for birthdays, Father's Day, dorm move-ins, and new babies, especially Father's Gaze, Together Forever, and Comfort in Quiet.

What is anime-influenced portrait art?

It is original figurative painting that borrows expressive faces, bold color, and character energy from anime illustration, without copying any licensed franchise.

Devorah's style blends that visual language with acrylic painterliness. Pieces like Free Spirit and Jasmine appeal to fans of illustrated wall art who want something unique rather than mass-market posters.

How often is new art added?

Devorah adds work when she chooses to share it, not on a fixed schedule. Recent additions include The Woman on the Bus (July 2026). The homepage and sitemap update when new pieces go live.

Not everything she paints appears on the site. Devorah keeps some pieces for herself on purpose, to protect her energy and avoid fatigue. Following her on social media (Pinterest and Reels are planned) is the best way to see when new shared work is on the way.
